Icon style

Lucide icons, 24px, 1.5px stroke weight. Used for pain point cards and UI elements.

Do

Use Lucide icons at 24px with 1.5px stroke. Keep icons monochrome (Ink or Ink Muted). Use icons to support text, not replace it.

Don't

Use filled icons, multicolor icons, emoji as icons, or icons from mixed icon sets. No stock illustration packs.

Do

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Keep transitions under 400ms for interactions. Stagger multi-element reveals by 75ms. Use animation to guide attention.

Don't

Use continuous animations (pulse, float, shimmer). No parallax scrolling. No scroll-jacking. No animations longer than 400ms for user-initiated actions.

07

Principles

Five rules that govern every design decision. Derived from GrowthX's philosophy: "show, don't decorate", "constraints enable creativity", "consistency compounds."

01

Earn every element

If it doesn't help the reader understand, navigate, or act, remove it. No decorative flourishes. No visual filler. White space is a feature.

02

Authority through restraint

Navy + white + one accent. Generous whitespace. Clean type hierarchy. These sites should feel like a respected industry publication, not a marketing landing page.

03

Consistent across industries, distinct within each

Layout, typography, and components are identical across all sites. Only the accent color, geometric pattern, and content change per industry. The system is the brand.

04

Reading-first

720px content width. 17px body text. 1.75 line height. These are long-form articles for people who will actually read them. Optimize for reading, not scanning.

05

Funnel without friction

CTAs present but never aggressive. The content itself is the conversion mechanism. If the content is good enough, readers will want to learn about GrowthX. Trust the work.

Do's and don'ts

Do

Use generous white space between sections. Body text 17px with 1.75 line-height. Playbook Navy for emphasis only. Stagger scroll reveals by 75ms. Include hover states on all interactive elements. Keep article content at 720px max-width.

Don't

Use more than 2 accent colors per view. Add decorative elements without purpose. Use animations longer than 400ms for interactions. Set body text below 15px. Use stock photos or generic AI imagery. Break the Navy + Warm White + Green palette.
