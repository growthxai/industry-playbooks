# First 90 Days: Distribution Plan

## Phase 1: Stealth (Days 1-30)

**Goal:** Calibrate content quality, get indexed, fix what's broken before anyone's watching.

- Publish all 20 articles per site (construction first, then roll)
- Submit sitemaps to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- Verify indexing daily for the first two weeks — flag any pages not picked up
- Run each article through Panzer's Brain manually and note recurring pipeline issues
- Fix writing guidelines and proofreader checklists based on what the pipeline gets wrong
- Monitor initial impressions and click data in Search Console — identify which queries are getting early traction
- Set up PostHog for on-site behavior tracking (scroll depth, time on page, cluster navigation patterns)
- Test all CTAs — email capture, "Talk to GrowthX" footer link, author card clicks
- No external promotion. No social posts. No links from GrowthX properties. Silent launch.

**Exit criteria:** All 20 articles live and indexed. Pipeline producing consistent 7+/10 Panzer's Brain scores. At least 50% of pages showing impressions in Search Console.

## Phase 2: LinkedIn Push (Days 31-60)

**Goal:** Coordinated company-wide LinkedIn distribution. Every GrowthX employee shares pre-formatted posts linking to playbook articles.

### Pre-work (Days 25-30, overlapping with Phase 1)

- Write 10-15 LinkedIn post templates per industry — short, opinionated, linking to one article each
- Each post follows the Marcel LinkedIn style: hook line, white space, one key insight, link
- Personalization slots so each employee can adjust ("I've been working with [industry] leaders..." or "Something I keep hearing from [persona]...")
- Create a shared doc or Notion page with all posts, organized by industry and article
- Brief the team: here's the playbook, here's why we're doing this, here are your posts, post 2-3 per week

### Execution (Days 31-60)

- Stagger posts across the team — no more than 2-3 GrowthX employees posting about the same article on the same day
- Prioritize posts from team members with relevant industry connections
- Matthew posts the anchor pieces — the ones with the strongest hooks and broadest appeal
- Track engagement (likes, comments, reposts) per post and per article to identify what resonates
- Iterate post templates based on what works — double down on formats that drive clicks
- Respond to every comment. This is relationship-building, not broadcasting.

## Phase 3: Broader Social + Email (Days 61-90)

**Goal:** Expand beyond LinkedIn. Integrate playbook content into the AI Led Growth newsletter and broader social channels.

### AI Led Growth Newsletter Integration

- Feature 1-2 playbook articles per newsletter issue as "Industry Spotlight" or "From the Playbooks" section
- Position as: "We built AI playbooks for [industry] leaders. Here's what we're learning."
- Link drives newsletter readers to the playbook sites — cross-pollination between the ALG audience and the industry-specific sites
- Track click-through from newsletter to playbook articles separately (UTM tags)

### Social expansion

- Repurpose top-performing LinkedIn posts to X/Twitter
- Create short-form derivatives: pull one stat or one insight per article, post as standalone
- If any articles gained organic search traction in Phase 1, amplify those specifically
- Test industry-specific hashtags and communities (e.g., construction Twitter, #CPA Twitter, #WealthManagement)

### Email capture follow-up

- By Day 60, the "Launching soon" newsletter should have collected early subscribers from the playbook sites
- Day 75: Send first industry newsletter to subscribers (even if the list is small)
- Newsletter cadence: biweekly to start, with 2-3 curated articles + one original insight
- Each newsletter drives back to the playbook site — the site is the hub, the newsletter is the reminder

## Return Visitors: Industry AI Skill-Up Courses (Phase 2+)

**Goal:** Give readers a reason to come back. The articles are the on-ramp. The courses are the retention mechanism.

### Concept

Each playbook site gets a structured "AI Skill-Up" course — a free, sequential learning path that takes a leader from zero AI experience to confidently using AI tools in their daily work. Not a video course. Not gated behind a paywall. A guided progression through the content, with progress tracking.

### Structure (per industry)

| Level | Name | Duration | What they learn |
|-------|------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | **AI Orientation** | 3 articles + 1 exercise | What AI is, what it isn't, where it fits in their industry. Complete one real task with an AI chat tool. |
| 2 | **Your First AI Workflow** | 4 articles + 2 exercises | Build a persistent AI workspace (Projects/Custom GPTs) for one recurring task. Save prompts, upload docs, get repeatable results. |
| 3 | **AI in Your Operations** | 4 articles + 2 exercises | Apply AI across multiple workflows — proposals, reporting, client comms. Measure time saved. |
| 4 | **Building with AI** | 4 articles + 3 exercises | Code-level automation. Build a pipeline that runs without manual intervention. For the reader or their technical staff. |

### Implementation

- Courses are built from existing playbook articles, sequenced into a guided path
- Each level unlocks after completing the previous (or the reader can skip ahead)
- Progress is tracked per visitor (local storage initially, accounts later if warranted)
- Each exercise is a concrete task: "Open Claude/ChatGPT. Paste this prompt. Upload your [document type]. Review the output."
- Completion of all 4 levels earns a downloadable "AI Ready" badge for the reader's LinkedIn profile (lightweight credential, signals to their network)

### Why this works for return visits

- Progress creates commitment — people come back to finish what they started
- Sequential structure means each visit has a clear "next step"
- Exercises require real work the reader does in their own business — not hypothetical
- The badge gives them something to show for it, which also promotes the playbook site organically
- Newsletter can nudge: "You're 60% through the Construction AI Skill-Up. Here's your next lesson."

### Timeline

- Design course structure during Phase 1 (stealth)
- Map existing articles to course levels during Phase 2 (LinkedIn push)
- Launch courses in Phase 3 alongside newsletter
- Additional course-specific articles produced by pipeline as gaps are identified
